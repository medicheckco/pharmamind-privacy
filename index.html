PharmaMind privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

PharmaMind is a study app for pharmacy students. Lessons work offline and your study progress stays on your phone.

What the app stores

Your study progress (topics done, quiz scores, flashcard schedule, bookmarks, mistakes and exam attempts) and your settings (course, Tamil help, theme and daily goal).

Where it is stored

Only in the app's private storage on your phone. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Your study progress is never sent to us or to anyone else.

What we collect

We do not receive, share or sell your personal data. The only data that leaves the phone is the Google Play Billing data described below.

Purchases

If you buy PharmaMind Pro, Google Play handles the payment. The app only asks Google Play whether Pro was bought on your Google account so it can unlock it, including after a reinstall. Google's Play Billing library uses the internet and sends Google your purchase history and diagnostic information so it can process and verify purchases; Google's privacy policy covers this. No study data is sent with it.

Deleting data

Settings → Reset progress removes all study data. Uninstalling the app also removes everything.

Changes

If a future version adds optional features such as sign-in, sync or downloadable content updates, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and those features will stay optional.

Educational use

PharmaMind is for educational use only. It is not medical advice and must not be used for diagnosis, treatment or dosing decisions.

Children

The app is meant for college students and is not aimed at children.
